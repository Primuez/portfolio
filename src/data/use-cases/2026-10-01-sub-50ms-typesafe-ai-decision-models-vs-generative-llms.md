## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Sub-50ms typesafe AI decision models are deterministic, schema-enforced inference pipelines engineered to execute classification, routing, and operational decisions without the latency, token overhead, or hallucination risks of autoregressive generative LLMs. By combining static typing contracts (such as Zod, Pydantic, or Protocol Buffers) with optimized edge architectures (ONNX, distilled SLMs, or gradient-boosted trees), these engines guarantee runtime execution within a 50-millisecond threshold. This dual-paradigm architecture provides mission-critical reliability for high-frequency autonomous workflows, fraud detection, and transactional automation where generative unpredictability is catastrophic.

1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers, immediately verified and cast through strict compile-time typesafe schemas to strip non-conforming tokens.
