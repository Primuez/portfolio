1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Webhooks detect and capture incoming reviews via the Google My Business API, sanitizing text tokens, extracting rating vectors, and parsing user metadata into standardized JSON payloads.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration pipelines analyze sentiment depth, evaluate customer intent, flag negative reviews for automated internal alerting, and match review subjects to verified location-specific SEO entities.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Output generation layers cross-reference responses with brand safety policies, embed localized long-tail keyphrases, and publish final replies through authenticated API webhooks with zero human intervention.
